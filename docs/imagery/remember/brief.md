# Remember still: brief

**Purpose:** the full-screen photograph that opens the Remember chapter, after the Read chapter ends on adding a word. It is the next morning in the same apartment, and the words come back. Over it: "You choose 10, 20 or 30 new words a day."

**Prompt:** the same shared look as `docs/imagery/hero/brief.md` Round 4, in the same apartment as `hero/r4-wide-2.png` and `stuck/r2-wide-2.png`, the next morning. Grey early daylight through the same window, an overcast northern sky, the lamp off and the room still dim. The same table by the window: the old book closed, the dictionary closed, a mug of tea. The same man, seen from behind, dark sweater, short dark hair, standing by the window with his phone held up near his face as if quietly answering it. The screen faces him, away from the camera. Small in the frame. The left third and the lower third fall into shadow, quiet enough for white words. In the tall, he and the window sit in the upper half, and the lower half is dark floor.

**Reject on sight, added to the hero's list:** bright or sunny daylight, a visible screen, a face or profile, a tidy showroom table, a new room that doesn't match the references.

## Round 1 critique

None picked. The light is right in all eight: soft grey daylight, the lamp off, a dark left wall and, in the talls, a long dark floor for the words. Two things fail:

- **He is on a phone call.** In every candidate the phone is held to his ear, which reads as a call, not as answering a review. The review is said out loud from memory, so he should hold the phone low, at his waist, its screen toward him, while he looks out of the window.
- **The room is the bare room the hero is leaving behind.** It must match the round 5 hero, once picked: the same lived-in details, wherever this camera can see them.

`r1-wide-2` has the best frame for words (him small at the right, a long quiet wall) and `r1-tall-2` the best tall. Both go to round 2 as composition references, with the round 5 hero for the room.

## Round 2 critique

Picked `r2-wide-4` and `r2-tall-4`, pending the owner's look. Round 2 attached the round 5 hero (`r5-wide-1`, `r5-tall-2`) for the room and round 1's `r1-wide-2` and `r1-tall-2` for the frame.

- **The pose reads now.** He stands at the window with his head up, toward the street, and holds the phone low in one hand. Only its back shows, with the camera lenses; the screen faces him.
- **The room matches the hero.** It has the worn rug, the second chair with the coat, the bag, the low shelf of old books, and on the table the lamp (off), the mug and the closed book. The light is grey early daylight, and the room stays dim.
- **Lettering.** At full size the spines are plain.
- **Room for words.** The left two-thirds and the floor stay dark enough for white words.
- **Not picked.** `r2-wide-1` hides the phone behind his hand. `r2-tall-3` hangs socks on the radiator and moves the coat. Codex returned 1664 × 936 this time, a hair off 1672 × 941, which `object-fit: cover` absorbs.
