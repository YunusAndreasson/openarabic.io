# Stuck still: brief

**Purpose:** the full-screen photograph behind the problem, straight after the unread line: "You can sound it out. You can't read it." The man from the first screen has stopped reading. The feeling is quiet defeat, not drama.

**Prompt:** the same shared look as `docs/imagery/hero/brief.md` Round 4, in the same dark apartment, later the same night. A man seen from behind and to one side, small in the frame, slumped at a small table by the window with his head resting on one hand. On the table: one open plain book pushed away, two thick open dictionaries, a few loose sheets of paper. One small lamp. The left third of the frame dark enough for white text. Every page blank or too far and soft to read. No lettering of any kind, no script, no calligraphy. The rejection list there applies.

## Round 1 critique

Picked `wide-2` and `tall-2`. The head in the hand reads at a glance, the left side and lower half hold the words, and it is plainly the hero's room later the same night. `wide-1` has a door edge intruding and the man is too small to read as stuck. `tall-1` shows the posture less clearly. All pages checked at full size: blank, with no lettering. One round, because the first was on target and the owner wanted the page today; `DESIGN.md`'s two-round rule is withdrawn with the rest of `Imagery`.

## Round 2: a table someone actually worked at

The owner, 2026-11-04: the concept is good, "but the way the books are placed makes it look like obvious ai". Diagnosis of `wide-2` and `tall-2`:

- Four open books on a table that holds two, all the same size, all lying flat and fanned towards the camera rather than towards him. He faces past them, into the wall.
- Every page is the same clean, glowing white, opened to the same spread, with no curve at the gutter and no weight.
- The loose sheets are fanned evenly, like a product shot. Nothing is stacked, closed, pushed aside, under something or cut off by the frame.

**Keep:** the room, the window on the wet street, the radiator, the lamp, the plant, the dark left third for the words, his posture with his head in his hand, the camera position and the 35 mm high-ISO look. `wide-2` and `tall-2` go to Codex as the reference.

**The table, as it would be after two hours:**

- One book he was reading: an old hardcover, open, directly in front of him under his free forearm, pages curling up towards the gutter, off-white and yellowed, half in the shadow of his shoulder.
- One thick dictionary, open, pulled close at his left, its spine cracked, a pencil lying in its gutter. Seen at an angle from the camera, not face on.
- A notebook with a few lines of pencil too small and soft to read, a second pencil, an eraser crumb or two.
- A closed paperback and the phone, face down, near the back edge. The mug pushed back by the lamp.
- Things overlap and lean: a book half on top of the notebook, a corner hanging over the table's edge, one object cut by his arm. Not every object is lit; the far end of the table goes into shadow.

**Reject on sight, added to the hero's list:** more than two open books; open books facing the camera; identical books or identical spreads; pages brighter than the lampshade; evenly fanned paper; anything arranged; any marks on a page that read as letters.

## Round 2 critique

Picked `r2-wide-2` for the wide. The table finally reads as used: the dictionary open with a pencil in its gutter and its edge over the table, a notebook sliding off the side, the closed books stacked, the phone face down, the mug pushed back. At 500% the pages hold only a soft grey block, with no letter shapes. `r2-wide-1` cured the pile of books but added a vase of flowers and a bowl of pastries, a styled still life, which is the same fault in a new form. Both tall candidates failed: `r2-tall-2` is the old table again, with three open books fanned to the camera, and `r2-tall-1` adds what reads as an ashtray.

## Round 3: the tall from the chosen wide

`r2-wide-2` goes to Codex as the only reference: the same table, the same objects in the same places, recomposed as a portrait with the man and table in the upper half and dark floor below. Nothing added to the table: no flowers, fruit, ashtray, candles or extra books.

## Round 3 critique

Picked `r3-tall-2`. Its table is `r2-wide-2`'s, object for object: the dictionary open with a pencil in its gutter, the notebook over the edge, the stack with a closed book on top, the phone face down, the mug by the lamp. So a visitor who turns a phone or resizes a window sees the same night. He is smaller than in round 1 and the lower 60% is dark floor, which holds the words at 390 px. `r3-tall-1` is also clean, but its notebook is closed and its table differs from the wide one. Pages in both hold only a soft grey line texture at 200%, with no letter shapes.
