# Stuck still: brief

**Purpose:** the full-screen photograph behind the problem, straight after the unread line: "You can sound it out. You can't read it." The man from the first screen has stopped reading. The feeling is quiet defeat, not drama.

**Prompt:** the same shared look as `docs/imagery/hero/brief.md` Round 4, in the same dark apartment, later the same night. A man seen from behind and to one side, small in the frame, slumped at a small table by the window with his head resting on one hand. On the table: one open plain book pushed away, two thick open dictionaries, a few loose sheets of paper. One small lamp. The left third of the frame dark enough for white text. Every page blank or too far and soft to read. No lettering of any kind, no script, no calligraphy. The rejection list there applies.
