I can tell

A present-shape verb with 'I' in its prefix -- 'I recognize/can tell'. The present frames it as a current perception, Abu Talhah reading the hunger in the Prophet's voice as he speaks.

in it

A 'in' preposition fused with '-hi' (it/him), pointing to the voice or the Prophet -- 'in it'. The preposition marks where the hunger is detected, and the attached pronoun supplies that locus in one word.

the hunger

The hunger he detects -- object of 'recognize', made definite by al- ('the hunger'). Its ending marks it as the thing the verb lands on, the trait he reads in the voice.

so is it

The fa- moves from observation to a follow-up -- 'and so...' -- carrying a yes/no question word that turns the next clause into a question. Together they ask 'so do you have anything?', the fa- linking it to the hunger just noticed.

with you

A 'at/with' preposition fused with '-ki' (your, female), the Arabic way of saying possession -- 'in your keeping' = 'you have'. The feminine suffix marks Umm Sulaym as the one addressed.

from

A 'from/any' preposition used to frame an indefinite query -- 'anything at all'. In a question like this it softens to 'any', and it governs the following noun into the genitive ending it assigns.

anything

The thing asked about -- 'anything' -- governed by the preceding 'any/from' and so in the genitive, with its -in tail marking it indefinite. It completes the open 'do you have any thing?' question.
