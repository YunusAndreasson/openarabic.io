and it was said

wa- ties this onto the prior saying, and the rest is a passive completed-action verb ('it was said') with no named sayer. The passive, marked by inner vowel change, keeps the source anonymous.

patience

A definite noun ('patience') standing as the subject of a verbless definition, in the nominative. The al- marks the known virtue being defined by the predicate that follows.

steadiness

A noun ('steadiness') serving as the predicate ('patience is steadiness') and the head of an 'of' pairing with the next word. The two nouns sit side by side with no linking verb.

the heart

The owner half of the 'of' chain ('steadiness of the heart'), a definite noun in the genitive. Its 'the' fixes the pairing, locating the steadiness in the heart.

in

A preposition of time/occasion ('at/in'), governing the following noun into the genitive. It marks WHEN the steadiness shows - at moments of disturbance.

moments

A broken-plural noun ('moments/occasions') leading an 'of' pairing, in the genitive after the preposition. As the head it stays bare of 'the' and hands definiteness to the owner that follows.

disturbance

A definite noun ('disturbance') as the owner half of the chain ('moments of disturbance'), in the genitive. Its 'the' fixes the pairing as definite, naming what the moments consist of.
