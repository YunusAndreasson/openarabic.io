# Concept pick, 2026-10-31

**Pick: A, One sentence.** Made by an agent, because the owner reviews only the final product. Reasoning, specimens and tests are in `docs/research/concepts.md`.

**This is a judgement, not evidence.** The emotion test in step 7 hasn't happened. No learner has seen any specimen, and an agent's opinion doesn't substitute for that. If the test fails, this goes back to step 5.

## Why A

- **It gives the visitor the moment.** The owner named it: reading a classical line without the English. A is the only concept where that happens on the page, by the visitor's own hand. They tap the words, then press a control that takes the English away.
- **The remembered image is one real object.** The same line appears twice and looks identical; only the reader has changed.
- **The text helps.** Ibn al-Jawzi: وَإِذَا رَأَيْتُ كِتَابًا لَمْ أَرَهُ فَكَأَنِّي وَقَعْتُ عَلَى كَنْزٍ، "When I saw a book I had not seen before, it was as if I had stumbled upon a treasure." A scholar's delight in a book, for someone who can't yet read one, and it ends on "a treasure". It's also neither Qur'an nor hadith.
- **It needs the fewest claims outside the ledger** and has the lowest honesty risk.
- **It's cheap.** No script, no pin, no image needed at the moment of pay-off.

## What was rejected

- **B, A thousand years.** It gives the moment to history rather than the visitor, makes the first screen depend on knowing the Hijri calendar, and needs years outside the ledger. Placing an app at the end of a line of scholars sounds grand and claims a succession nothing supports. A small version number could still appear as a footnote, because the owner confirmed that motif is deliberate. اقْرَأْ isn't used anywhere.
- **C, The first year.** Its structure implies progress by a date, close to what the ledger bans, and no data shows how fast a real learner's horizon grows. The test account's capture has 0 words at "a year+". The memory horizon itself stays a strong true claim for a later screen.

## Risks in the pick

- **The pay-off is quiet.** If nobody notices the line's return, the page has no climax. Only cold viewing can say.
- **Reading nine words after tapping them is a small feat.** A careful visitor may feel it as a trick rather than as reading.
- **"Take the English away" might read as anti-translation or as an app feature.** The next screen says the app keeps its English, but the words are draft and need testing.
- **Word colour on a real iPhone is unproven,** including the one-colour path below iOS 26.2.
- **Ledger or attribution trouble could remove the line.** The article around 7106 is mostly a biographer's voice, and 7106 hasn't been checked against an edition. Published content has other problems, listed in `concepts.md`.
